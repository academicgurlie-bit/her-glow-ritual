✨ Weekly & Monthly Reminders

Monthly

  • Baby Foot Peel — once a month. Booties on for an hour, seal with socks, peeling starts in 3–5 days. Plan it for a week you're mostly home. Pairs with a Sunday soft-reset night.
  • Moroccan Hammam Swap — first Saturday of the month, in place of the regular deep-clean night.
  • 💗 PDRN / Red Pad Hydration Reset — approximately once a month or less, on a recovery night (Tue/Thu/Sat). Never on an active night — if it coincides with one, the scheduled active/recovery plan wins.

Weekly Skin Focus

  • 🧴 Monday — Epiduo Night
  • 💧 Tuesday — Recovery Night
  • 🧴 Wednesday — Adapalene 0.1% Night
  • 💧 Thursday — Recovery Night
  • ✨ Friday — Paula's Choice 2% BHA Night
  • 💧 Saturday — Recovery Night
  • 🛡️ Sunday — Barrier Night (Dr. Althea 345 + Cicaplast as extra barrier support)

One treatment per night, never stacked. Recovery nights are for rest — no acids, no retinoids. Modes change the amount of routine, never the treatment schedule.

Hair Removal / Ingrown Reminder System

  1. Exfoliate the day before
  2. Trim on clean, dry skin
  3. Rinse cool, pat dry
  4. Tend Skin once fully dry
  5. Fragrance-free moisturizer that night
  6. First Aid Beauty Ingrown Pads starting the next day, 2–3x/week
  7. Never Tend Skin + Ingrown Pads in the same session
  8. Veet only as tolerated — patch test, never on irritated skin
  9. Epilator = experimental, start small

Scent System (when fragrance is appropriate)

  1. Vaseline on pulse points — wrists, inner elbows, behind ears, throat, chest, behind knees. Neutral and occlusive — traps fragrance instead of letting it evaporate within the hour.
  2. Perfume oil on top — dab, never rub (rubbing breaks the scent molecules apart and fades it faster)
  3. Body mist last, optional — your top note riding over the oil's base note
  4. Scented powder once skin is fully dry — light dust on friction zones only (underboob, inner thighs, between cheeks)

Perfume oil menu: Sand + Fog Vanilla Sands (everyday default), Vanilla Musk (evenings), Strawberry Spritz (daytime), Choco Musk (rich/gourmand), the Lebanese oil (your wildcard), Forvr Mood She Was Here (special occasions, running low), BBW Warm Vanilla Sugar mist (top layer only, not a pulse-point oil).

Intimate Care — Golden Rules

  • Vulva = external, vagina = internal. External skin gets hygiene and care. The internal environment cleans itself — no douching, no internal cleansing, ever.
  • No vaginal deodorant, no perfume inside, no scented products on sensitive tissue. Normal, natural scent is healthy scent.
  • Lume, external only, is the whole cleansing routine — no regular soap, no fragrance, no complicated steps.
  • This isn't meant to be a project. Clean, comfortable, fresh, healthy — that's the whole bar.
  • Post-shave rule: never tranexamic acid or exfoliate right after shaving. Wait 24 hours — shave day is Lume + moisturizer only.

Before You Buy — 5 Questions

  1. Do I already own something that does this job?
  2. Does the new one perform meaningfully better, not just prettier?
  3. Will I actually use it, realistically?
  4. Is it worth the extra cost over what I have?
  5. Am I buying it because it works — or because it's cute?

What to Expect — Realistic Timeline

  • Week 1–2: Heels + skin already feel different post-shower. Barrier strengthening, lash serums depositing at the follicle — you feel the work before anyone sees it.
  • Month 1–2: Texture visibly smoother, tone evening out, scent system fully calibrated. People start saying "you look good lately" with no explanation why.
  • Month 3–4: Lash length undeniable, hyperpigmentation faded, even tone everywhere, intimate area fully compounded.
  • Month 6+: Full transformation — skin, lashes, body all compounded. Bare face mogs.
